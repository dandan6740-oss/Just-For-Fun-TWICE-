THE STORY

More than a girl group.

01

TWICE is a nine-member South Korean girl group formed by JYP Entertainment through the survival show SIXTEEN.

They officially debuted on October 20, 2015 with the mini album THE STORY BEGINS and the title track “Like OOH-AHH.” Their name carries a simple idea: touching people twice — once through the ears with great music and again through the eyes with outstanding performances.
